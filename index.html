About

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the National University of Singapore, advised by Yifan Feng.

My research studies learning and decision-making in strategic environments, with a focus on how platforms and markets can design effective policies when participants respond strategically to incentives and algorithms. I develop models and algorithms using tools from online learning and game theory, with applications to digital platforms, marketplaces, auctions, and financial markets.
